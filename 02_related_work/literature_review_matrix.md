# Literature Review Matrix

Below is the literature review matrix analyzing the 7 fundamental research papers used as the scientific foundation to design and evaluate the RAG-based LMS for CLO-aligned quiz generation and personalized feedback:

| No | Paper Title | Authors | Year | Venue | Domain | AI Method | Dataset | Metrics | Main Contribution | Limitation | Relevance to Our Topic |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | KAQG: A Knowledge-Graph-Enhanced RAG for Difficulty-Controlled Question Generation | Ching Han Chen, Ming Fang Shiu | 2025 | arXiv / IEEE TechRxiv | Difficulty-Controlled Education | Multi-agent Graph-RAG + PageRank + IRT (3PL) + Bloom Taxonomy + DDS | 102 PDF textbooks & 3 ACT Reading passages | Accuracy (P-value), Discrimination Index, Expert Rating, Parameter recovery (Corr, RMSE, Misclassification rate) | A difficulty-controlled question generation framework that maps knowledge graph attributes (PageRank, depth) and Bloom's Taxonomy into the 3PL IRT model. | Complex multi-agent DDS architecture, making it hard to deploy for lightweight LMS. Needs wide-scale empirical calibration. | Defines how to control cognitive difficulty levels (Bloom) and select core concepts (PageRank) from lecture slides. |
| 2 | Leveraging In-Context Learning and Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Automatic Question Generation in Educational Domains | Subhankar Maity, Aniket Deroy, Sudeshna Sarkar | 2025 | arXiv | Multi-domain School Education | Hybrid Model (FAISS RAG + GPT-4 ICL few-shot) | EduProbe (3,502 Q&A pairs from NCERT textbooks grade 6-12) | BLEU-4, ROUGE-L, METEOR, ChRF, BERTScore, Likert scale (Gramm, Appr, Rel, Comp, Answ), Fleiss's Kappa | Proposes a hybrid QA generation framework (FAISS RAG + GPT-4 few-shot ICL) that achieves the highest quality in cognitive complexity and relevance. | Retrieval noise might reduce answerability. Evaluation was limited to Indian secondary school textbooks (NCERT). | Provides the technical pipeline for advanced hybrid quiz generation (Advanced RAG + Few-shot prompting). |
| 3 | Enhancing Automated Exam Creation with Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Scalable Educational Assessment | Charaf Hamidi, Mohamed Badiy, Salma Gaou, Fatima Amounas, Mourade Azrour, Hicham Tribak, Abdullah M. Alnajim, Abdulatif Alabdulatif | 2025 | Journal of Advances in Information Technology (JAIT) | Higher Education (SQL Database Course) | RAG + Next.js + OLLaMA (LLaMA 3.2) + FAISS (`all-MPNet-base-v2`) + Zero-shot Re-ranking (`bart-large-mnli`) | 102 PDF French SQL textbooks (~75MB text, 2520 chunks) | Retrieval Latency (s), Precision@5, Expert Review, Time Saving (%) | Builds a Next.js web LMS for automated SQL exam generation with advanced RAG pipeline (chunk cleaning, zero-shot re-ranking) and a human-in-the-loop review process. | Only supports French and SQL databases. Evaluated in small-scale classrooms; lacks automated RAG evaluation (like Ragas). | Serves as a reference for actual web LMS design, the instructor's review panel, and re-ranking techniques. |
| 4 | Generating In-Context, Personalized Feedback for Intelligent Tutors with Large Language Models | Jennifer M. Reddig, Arav Arora, Christopher J. MacLellan | 2025 | Springer (Int. Journal of AI in Education) | Mathematics (College Algebra) | LLM Prompting (GPT-4 CoT) + Simulated Student Evaluation | 6,926 learning transactions (1,307 erroneous responses from Apprentice Tutors) | Accuracy of error diagnosis (%), Hint Helpful rating, Simulated student pass rate (%), Cohen's Kappa | Integrates GPT-4 to diagnose 5 types of student errors and automatically generate personalized corrective feedback in Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS). | 35% of hints are too general or leak the answer. Evaluation on simulated students showed a low pass rate (35%); needs human-in-the-loop. | Provides error classification models and prompt design logic for personalized feedback when students fail a quiz. |
| 5 | Automated Educational Question Generation at Different Bloom's Skill Levels using Large Language Models: Strategies and Evaluation | Nicy Scaria, Suma Dharani Chenna, Deepak Subramani | 2024 | arXiv / AIED 2024 | Graduate Data Science Education | Zero/Few-shot LLM Prompting + CoT (Mistral 7B, Llama 2 70B, Palm 2, GPT-3.5, GPT-4, Gemini Pro) | DataScienceQ (2,550 questions across 17 topics) | 9-item Hierarchical Rubric, PINC score, Cohen's Kappa, Quadratic Weighted Kappa | Evaluates 5 LLMs across 5 prompting strategies for Bloom-based quiz generation; proves that Chain-of-Thought with definitions and examples (PS4) performs best. | Relies solely on LLM internal knowledge (no RAG), leading to hallucinations. Gemini Pro's automated evaluation correlates poorly with experts. | Guides prompt engineering to generate CLO-aligned questions and provides a 9-item quality evaluation rubric. |
| 6 | Using Focus to Personalise Learning and Feedback in Software Engineering Education | Bansri Amish Modi, Andrew Cain, Guy Wood-Bradley, Jake Renzella | 2023 | IEEE/ACM (ICSE-SEET 2023) | Software Engineering Education | Focus Framework for learning objectives tracking | Course Tasks mapping to CLOs | Focus-based Personalization Rate, Student Reflection efficiency | Proposes the "Focus" framework to model learning objectives and relate feedback across software engineering tasks to track CLO performance. | Initial mapping of CLOs to specific tasks requires significant effort. Evaluated in a limited number of class cohorts. | Establishes the SE education context where personalized feedback and CLO alignment are highly demanded. |
| 7 | Lecturers' adoption to use the online Learning Management System (LMS): Empirical evidence from TAM2 model for Vietnam | Nguyen Huu Khoa | 2020 | HCMCOU Journal of Science | Higher Education in Vietnam | TAM2 model for Technology Acceptance | Survey dataset from Vietnamese universities | Perceived Usefulness, Perceived Ease of Use, Lecturer Workload, Intention to Adopt | Identifies key factors influencing lecturers' adoption of online LMS platforms in Vietnam using the TAM2 model. | Survey-based study, does not evaluate technical enhancements (like AI integration) to resolve adoption barriers. | Highlights the instructor's workload bottleneck in preparing digital contents (like quizzes), supporting the motivation for AI LMS. |
