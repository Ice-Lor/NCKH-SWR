# Literature Review Matrix

| Paper | Domain | AI Model / Method | Dataset | Evaluation Metrics | Main Contribution | Limitation | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper 1** | HR / Tech Recruiting | Multimodal Multi-Agent (GPT-4o, Whisper, PaddleOCR, Docling) | 64 real resumes (Python backend engineers) | Precision, Recall, Confusion Matrix, $T_{avg}$, Cost ($) | Developed a multimodal multi-agent hiring assistant with traceable rationales and time-quality metric ($T_{avg}$) | Dependent on LLM orchestration stability; small domain-specific dataset | **High** |
| **Paper 2** | HR / Talent Analytics | Knowledge Graphs (Neo4j), NLP, NER (SpaCy), Word2Vec, GloVe, Sentiment Analysis | 1,000 real resumes (15 industries, 25 countries) | Skill Extraction (P, R, F1), Sentiment Acc, Ranking (Top 3/5/10) | Represented candidate skill depth via sentiment-enhanced Knowledge Graphs; enabled complex querying | Requires a manually constructed baseline gazetteer/ontology to initialize graph weights | **High** |
| **Paper 3** | HR / CV Matching | Hybrid NLP: Classifier, RegEx, Fuzzy Matching, all-MiniLM-L6-v2, Docling, pdfplumber | 1,000+ resumes & hundreds of JDs (bilingual French/English) | Extraction Acc (HR feedback), Top-3 match rate | Created end-to-end explainable matching pipeline with a visual rationale layer and specialized name classifier | Fails on highly complex multi-column layouts; modest dataset size | **High** |
| **Paper 4** | HR / Tech Recruiting | Multi-Agent Framework, LLM Rubrics, Active Listwise Tournament, Plackett–Luce (PL) Model | Multi-industry profiles & rubrics | NDCG@K (10%-25%), Kendall's τ, Utility movement (Δu) | Designed explainable multi-agent assessment; introduced Listwise Tournament via PL aggregation for global utilities | Performance bounded by input data quality; risk of rubric drift and bias amplification in active loops | **High** |
| **Paper 5** | HR Ethics / Compliance | Algorithmic Fairness Review: Pre/In/Post-processing, LLMs, ASR (Whisper), Multimodal | Multi-source literature, corporate data, bias case studies | 4/5th rule, Demographic/Accuracy/Predictive parity, Counterfactual fairness | Systematized a comprehensive taxonomy of hiring biases across 4 stages; categorized mathematical fairness metrics | Theoretical survey focusing on conceptual frameworks over engineering implementation | **High** |
| **Paper 6** | HR / Job Matching | Siamese Sentence-BERT (SBERT) | 270,000+ annotated resume-vacancy pairs | NDCG, MRR, Precision@K | Developed state-of-the-art multilingual and cross-lingual job matching via fine-tuned SBERT | High dependency on massive labeled human training datasets | **High** |
| **Paper 7** | General NLP | TSDAE (Transformer-based Sequential Denoising Auto-Encoder) | 4 benchmark datasets (AskUbuntu, CQADupStack, SciDocs, TwitterPara) | MAP, MAP@100, AP, Spearman's $\rho$, Reconstruction Loss | Introduced TSDAE using a reconstruction bottleneck; optimized domain adaptation and pre-training | Prone to overfitting with pre-trained encoder-decoder models (BART, T5); high reliance on nouns | **Low** <br>*(Chỉ đóng góp kỹ thuật NLP cơ bản)* |
| **Paper 8** | HR / Talent Management | Survey: Supervised/Unsupervised, Deep Learning (GNN, LSTM), Generative AI (LLMs, Agents) | Internal (HRIS/ATS profiles, reviews) & External (OPNs/LinkedIn, Twitter) talent data | Classification (ACC, F1, AUC), Ranking, Regression, Generative (ROUGE, BLEU) | Provided a multi-level taxonomy (individual, organizational, market) of AI talent analytics; mapped future LLM Agent trends | Broad literature survey providing conceptual roadmaps rather than an empirical model | **High** |
| **Paper 9** | HR Tech / Transparency | Mixed-methods and database building framework (TARAI Index, Airtable) | 113 unique HR Tech products (interviews & conference lists) | Qualitative evaluations via 3 design workshops (Personas, Walkthroughs) | Conceptualized contextual AI transparency for HRM via the TARAI Index across functionality, claims, and assumptions | Bounded by the completeness of vendor website data; product-level scores obscure sub-feature nuances | **High** |
| **Paper 10** | HR / Interview Bias | Integrated pipeline: Whisper, ChatGPT, ElevenLabs, TextBlob | 10 simulated interviews (varied knowledge levels 1-5 & positive/negative tones) | Knowledge Level (1-5), Sentiment Analysis, AI vs. Human Rating | Built and tested an automated interview system to minimize sentiment bias, achieving a 41.2% reduction | Extremely small experimental dataset; not optimized for evaluation of soft-skill dependent roles | **High** |
