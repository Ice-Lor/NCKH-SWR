# Literature Review Matrix

### BẢNG TỔNG HỢP TÀI LIỆU THAM KHẢO (LITERATURE REVIEW SYNTHESIS)

| Paper | Domain | AI Model / Method | Dataset | Evaluation Metrics | Main Contribution | Limitation | Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper 1** | HR / Tech Recruitment | Multi-agent framework orchestrated by GPT-4o, GPT-4o Vision, Whisper, PaddleOCR, Docling | 64 real resumes of mid-level Python backend engineers (compared against 2 human recruiters) | Precision, Recall, Confusion Matrix, Efficiency Metric ($T_{avg}$), Screening Cost ($) | Developed a multimodal multi-agent hiring assistant with traceable rationales; proposed a time-quality efficiency metric ($T_{avg}$) | High dependence on orchestrating LLM stability; small dataset limited to one engineering role | **High** |
| **Paper 2** | HR / Talent Analytics | Knowledge Graphs (Neo4j), NLP Parsing, NER (SpaCy), Word Embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe), Sentiment Analysis | 1,000 real resumes across 15 industries and 25 countries | Skill Extraction (Precision, Recall, F1-Score), Sentiment Accuracy, Ranking Accuracy (Top 3, 5, 10) | Represents candidate skill depth via sentiment-enhanced Knowledge Graphs; enables multi-criteria complex skill querying | Requires a manually constructed baseline gazetteer/ontology to initialize graph weights | **High** |
| **Paper 3** | HR / CV Matching | Hybrid NLP: Supervised Classifier, RegEx, Fuzzy Matching, all-MiniLM-L6-v2, Docling, pdfplumber | 1,000+ resumes and hundreds of JDs (primarily bilingual French/English from IT & Telecom) | Information Extraction accuracy (via HR expert feedback), Top-3 match rate | End-to-end explainable matching pipeline with a visual rationale layer; specialized name extraction model | Structurally fails on highly complex multi-column layouts; modest dataset size | **High** |
| **Paper 4** | HR / Tech Recruitment | Multi-Agent Architecture, LLMs (Rubric Generation), Active Listwise Tournament, Plackett–Luce (PL) Model | Fixed pool of real candidate materials across tech and leadership roles | NDCG@K (K=10% to 25%), Convergence stability (Kendall-τ, Utility movement Δu) | Developed an explainable multi-agent assessment framework; introduced an Active Listwise Tournament layer using Plackett-Luce aggregation to build globally stable candidate utilities | Performance is strictly bounded by input data quality; risk of rubric drift and bias amplification across active learning loops | **High** |
| **Paper 5** | HR Ethics / Compliance | Algorithmic fairness review: Pre/In/Post-processing, LLMs (GPT-4, Gemini), ASR (Whisper), Multimodal systems | Multi-source literature, corporate historical datasets, and notable bias case studies | Four-fifths rule, Demographic parity, Equality of Opportunity, Predictive rate parity, Counterfactual & Individual fairness, Regression fairness | Systematic taxonomy of biases across all 4 recruitment stages; detailed classification of mathematical fairness metrics and auditing practices | Theoretical survey focusing on categorization and conceptual frameworks over engineering implementation | **High** |
| **Paper 6** | HR / Job Matching | Siamese Sentence-BERT (SBERT) | 270,000+ resume-vacancy pairs (annotated) | NDCG, MRR, Precision@K | State-of-the-art multilingual matching via fine-tuned SBERT | High dependency on massive labeled human training data | **High** |
| **Paper 7** | General NLP | TSDAE (Transformer-based Sequential Denoising Auto-Encoder), Encoder-Decoder Framework | AskUbuntu, CQADupStack, TwitterPara, SciDocs, Wikipedia corpus | MAP, MAP@100, Average Precision (AP), Spearman's correlation, Reconstruction Loss | Introduced TSDAE, a novel unsupervised sentence embedding method using a reconstruction bottleneck; optimized domain adaptation & pre-training | Suffers from overfitting when initialized with pre-trained encoder-decoder models (BART, T5); high reliance on nouns as core content cues | **Low** <br>*(Chỉ đóng góp kỹ thuật NLP cơ bản, không có tính ứng dụng HR)* |
| **Paper 8** | HR / Talent Management | Comprehensive AI Taxonomy Survey: Supervised/Unsupervised, Deep Learning (GNN, LSTM), Generative AI (LLMs, Agents) | Extensive academic literature, historical HRIS/ATS profiles, Glassdoor reviews, and social media text | Classification (ACC, F1, AUC), Ranking (NDCG, Precision@N), Regression (RMSE, MAE, MAPE), Generative (ROUGE, BLEU) | Provided an up-to-date, comprehensive taxonomy of AI in talent analytics across individual, organizational, and market levels; mapped future LLM Agent trends | Broad literature survey providing conceptual roadmaps and multi-level framework categorizations rather than a new empirical model | **High** |
| **Paper 9** | HR Tech / Contextual AI Transparency | Mixed-methods and database building framework (TARAI Index with Airtable views) | 113 unique HR Tech products from recruiter interviews and conference exhibitor lists | Qualitative evaluations via 3 design workshops (Personas, Walkthroughs) | Conceptualized contextual AI transparency for HRM via the TARAI Index across functionality, claims, assumptions, and clarity; provided a participatory database model | Strictly bounded by the availability of public vendor website data; product-level clarity scores hide sub-feature transparency nuances | **High** |
| **Paper 10** | HR / Interview Bias Mitigation | Integrated pipeline: Whisper, ChatGPT, ElevenLabs, TextBlob | Controlled experimental data from 10 simulated interviews | Knowledge Level (1-5), Sentiment Analysis, AI vs. Human Rating | Built and tested a novel automated interview system to minimize sentiment bias, achieving a 41.2% reduction | Extremely small experimental dataset; not suitable for evaluating jobs heavily dependent on emotional skills | **High** |
