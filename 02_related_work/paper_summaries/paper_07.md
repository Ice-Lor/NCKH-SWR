# Paper 07 Summary

## Citation

- **Title:** Lecturers' adoption to use the online Learning Management System (LMS): Empirical evidence from TAM2 model for Vietnam
- **Authors:** Nguyen Huu Khoa
- **Year:** 2020
- **Source:** HCMCOU Journal of Science (Economics and Business Administration)
- **DOI/Link:** [https://doi.org/10.46223/HCMCOUJS.econ.en.10.1.216.2020](https://doi.org/10.46223/HCMCOUJS.econ.en.10.1.216.2020)

## Problem

While LMS platforms are widely introduced in higher education institutions in developing countries like Vietnam, actual adoption and active use by lecturers remain low. The barriers include technical difficulties, lack of alignment with pedagogical needs, and increased workload for preparing digital content.

## Method

The study utilizes the Technology Acceptance Model 2 (TAM2) to investigate factors affecting Vietnamese lecturers' intention to adopt and use online LMS platforms. It collects empirical data from lecturers across various universities in Vietnam.

## Context

Higher education institutions in Vietnam, specifically focusing on e-learning and LMS adoption challenges.

## Key Findings

1. Factors such as subjective norms, job relevance, output quality, and result demonstrability significantly influence lecturers' perceived usefulness and intention to adopt the LMS.
2. Lecturer workload in creating and managing content is a major bottleneck.

## Limitations

The study is limited to the survey dataset from specific Vietnamese universities and does not evaluate technical enhancements (like AI integrations) that could resolve these adoption barriers.

## Relevance to our topic

It provides the regional and institutional context (higher education in Vietnam) for LMS adoption. It highlights that lecturers face significant workload issues when creating course materials (like quizzes and feedback), which directly supports our motivation to design a requirements engineering framework for an AI/RAG-driven LMS that automates quiz generation and saves time.
